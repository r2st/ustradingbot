How to read this scan

A golden cross prints when the 20-day simple moving average rises above the 50-day — a classic sign that short-term momentum has turned up relative to the medium-term trend. A death cross is the opposite: the 20-day falling below the 50-day signals weakening momentum.

Fresh crosses (within the last few sessions) matter most — the older a cross, the more of the move has already happened. Confirm any crossover with volume before acting, and size entries with the position size calculator.
