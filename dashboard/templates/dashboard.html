T

Manual Trade Entry

Place a trade by hand — buy (long) or sell (short) any symbol with multiple stop-loss and profit-target levels for partial exits (e.g. stop 1 at −3% for 33%, stop 2 at −5% for 33%, stop 3 at −8% for the rest). Each level exits its slice of the position; the last level of each ladder takes whatever remains. Give a trigger as an absolute price or a % move from entry. Multi-level and short orders run on the paper broker; a simple long (one stop + one target) also works on IBKR. Requires the admin password.
